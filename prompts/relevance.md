# What counts as relevant

For now, pick the startup news a founder or early-stage operator would most want to know about today:

- Notable funding rounds, acquisitions, and IPO filings
- Significant product launches from startups
- Shifts in the venture market, and major founder or investor moves
- Regulation or platform changes that materially affect startups

Skip listicles, sponsored posts, opinion pieces with no new information, and minor press releases.
